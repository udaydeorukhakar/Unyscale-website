How It Works

From order to outcome -
intelligent at every step

Every manufacturing or service business follows the same pattern: something comes in, gets processed, and goes out. Unyscale makes every stage of that journey smarter - capturing data automatically, surfacing problems early, and helping you decide what to do next.

1
Order or intake arrives
Email, form, WhatsApp, or manual, AI captures and structures it
2
Resource planning assigned
Order backlog assigned across rolling months, capacity balanced before work begins
3
Bill of Materials (BOM) generated
Materials, costs, timelines - derived from the order, not built from scratch
4
Operations tracked live
Production, services, procurement - visible in real time on one dashboard
5
Problems caught early
Deadline risk, cost overruns, stock shortfalls - flagged before they happen
6
Decisions made on data
What-if simulation tells you the outcome before you commit

About Unyscale

Why we exist

India's manufacturing and service SMBs generate rich operational data every day — orders, production, procurement, finances. But that data sits locked away, accessible only after a week of Excel work or a call to the CA. Decisions get made on instinct, not information.

We didn't set out to replace existing systems. We set out to make them intelligent. The AI layer that captures documents, tracks operations, surfaces problems early, simulates decisions, and guides workers — built specifically for the way Indian SMBs actually work.

Our platform is in live production deployment with a manufacturing client in Thane — that's our proof of concept and the foundation for a platform that scales across garment, dairy, medical, logistics, and any business that runs on Tally, Zoho, or similar systems.
